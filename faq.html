FAQ

Common questions, answered plainly.

Timelines, revisions, ownership and support — the things people ask before they start.

How long does delivery take?

Landing pages take 7–10 days. Most multi-page websites launch in 2–4 weeks depending on final scope and how ready your content is.

Will it work on all devices?

Yes. Mobile, tablet and desktop responsiveness are validated during QA before launch.

Do you build Android apps (APK)?

Yes. Share your use case and we’ll propose a practical Android development plan with a matching website identity and maintenance support.

Can we start small and scale later?

Absolutely. Start with the key pages, then add pages and features in planned phases — the architecture is modular.

Who owns the code?

You do. You own the final code and assets once the project is complete.

Can you maintain my existing website?

Yes. We offer maintenance for updates, performance improvements and issue fixes, even for sites we didn’t build.

How are revisions handled?

Feedback is collected in structured rounds so speed and quality stay balanced. Reasonable revision rounds are included in your plan.

How does pricing work?

We recommend a budget after a short consultation, based on scope and timeline. Everything is explained before development starts.

Still have questions?

We’re happy to help — send a message and we’ll reply within 24 hours.
